Ocean Threats

Plastic Pollution

Plastic bags can be deadly when mistaken for food by marine animals.

Oil Spills

Oil coats marine animals' fur and feathers, harming their insulation and buoyancy.

Climate change

Warming waters bleach coral reefs and disrupt ocean ecosystems.
